ABOUT ME

My journey into programming started back in 2009 at Fundação Nokia de Ensino, a social project in the state of Amazonas, Brazil. From Pascal to C++ to ActionScript I fell in love early on with the power of building systems from scratch.

Since 2015 I've built and maintained software for Brazil's judiciary as a civil servant: Java and Ionic apps at the Court of Justice of Amazonas, Angular/Spring Boot systems and product ownership of a national budget system at the Regional Labor Court, and, today, BI dashboards and Python data pipelines at the Federal Court of Pernambuco.

I'm now applying that experience to AI: evaluating and improving model outputs for code, data and Portuguese–English tasks. I'm also earning a degree in Data Science and exploring machine learning and agentic AI in my own projects.

Outside of work, I recharge in nature with my family, play chess online, and read — technical or otherwise. I also play a bit of guitar and piano, and somewhere in a drawer there's a novel I'm slowly writing. Maybe before I hit 60 it'll see the light of day.
