ABOUT ME

My journey into programming started back in 2009 at Fundação Nokia de Ensino, a social project in the state of Amazonas, Brazil. From Pascal to C++ to ActionScript I fell in love early on with the power of building systems from scratch.

Today, I work at the intersection of data, artificial intelligence and web development. I build analytical solutions that help organizations make better decisions, and I explore Machine Learning and Agentic AI - systems capable of reasoning and acting autonomously to solve real business problems.

I'm also increasingly excited about Web3 and decentralized applications — a space I see as the next frontier for creating trustless, transparent, and user-owned systems. Through Dedalus Business Insights, my consultancy, I bring together these areas to deliver practical digital solutions for clients.

Outside of work, I recharge in nature with my family, play chess online, and read — technical or otherwise. I also play a bit of guitar and piano, and somewhere in a drawer there's a novel I'm slowly writing. Maybe before I hit 60 it'll see the light of day.
